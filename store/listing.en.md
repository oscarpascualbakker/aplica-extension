# Store listing (en)

Store texts for the English listing on the Chrome Web Store and Microsoft Edge Add-ons (CV-509). The
name and the summary come from the manifest (`src/_locales/en/messages.json`, `extName` and
`extDescription`); the description is pasted into the panel of each store. The stores do not
interpret Markdown: the text of the block is pasted as it is.

## Name (75 characters maximum)

```text
Aplica: save job postings and see your fit
```

## Summary (132 characters maximum)

```text
Save job postings to your Aplica account in one click, check your fit and keep your CV and form answers at hand. Free.
```

## Description

```text
You see a job posting on LinkedIn, on InfoJobs or on a company's website and you do not want to lose it. You click the Aplica icon, check what it has read (job title, company, location, work mode, salary and description), correct whatever needs it and save it to your account. Saving is free.

The extension works with Aplica (aplica.cv), where you prepare each application with your career profile: fit report, CV tailored in Word and PDF, cover letter and message to the recruiter. You need an Aplica account.

WHAT IT DOES

Saving the posting. It reads the structured data of the page and, on LinkedIn, Infoempleo, Tecnoempleo, Greenhouse, Lever, SmartRecruiters, Teamtailor, Personio and Factorial, the page layout of the job board itself. If a page does not let itself be read, you select the text of the posting and it is saved all the same. If you already had it saved, it tells you.

Checking your fit. The quick fit check compares the posting with your profile and gives you 3 to 5 key requirements (covered, to confirm or missing) and a provisional verdict, so that you can decide whether it is worth preparing the application. From the extension only the free readings of the day are used.

Having your documents to hand. If you have already prepared the application in Aplica, you download the CV in Word or PDF and copy the cover letter, the message to the recruiter and each form answer without leaving the job board.

Filling in the form. On Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, SmartRecruiters, Workable, Teamtailor and Personio it writes your contact details and your answers into the fields. Sensitive questions (salary, nationality, work permit, disability and others) it leaves blank for you to answer. It never submits the form: you do that.

Marking the application as sent. When you finish the form and the confirmation page appears, it asks whether you want to move the application to "sent" in your Aplica tracker. If you say yes, it records the date and reminds you to follow up after 7 days.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO

It does not read any page until you click its icon, and then only that tab. It does not ask for access to any site when you install it; access to the forms of those seven systems is requested only if you turn on autofill, and you can remove it whenever you like.
It does not spend credits, delete anything or send applications.
It saves nothing to your account until you click "Save to Aplica" or confirm a change.
It has no analytics or third-party code. In your browser it stores only the connection key and your language.

Its code is free (GPL-3.0) and you can review it on GitHub. The privacy policy is at https://aplica.cv/en/legal/privacy.

It works on Chrome, Edge and Firefox.
```
